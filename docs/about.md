# About Gareth

I build software, design systems, and spend a fair amount of time pulling apart
technical problems to see where the sharp edges really are.

I have been programming and architecting across different companies for roughly
15 years. This site exists partly as a forcing function: a place to document what
I am learning, make half-finished ideas visible, and occasionally turn them into
something more coherent.

If something here feels a little rough around the edges, that is usually because it
is. I would rather publish a useful note while it is still warm than wait for it to
become a perfect article.
