# Why keep a lab notebook?

Most side projects do not fail because there are no ideas. They fail because every
idea is forced to clear the bar of becoming a polished project or a complete article.

That is a bad filter for learning.

A lab notebook gives ideas somewhere to live while they are still incomplete. It
also makes it easier to notice patterns: the same architectural irritation showing
up in different systems, the same tooling gap appearing in different projects, the
same half-formed implementation idea refusing to go away.

For this site, that means a simple flow:

1. Capture the rough idea in the lab.
2. Add just enough detail that future-me can do something with it.
3. Promote it into a fuller note only if it earns the effort.

If nothing else, it is a better alternative to rediscovering the same thought six
months later and pretending it is new.
